# Data Rights Request

**We respect your privacy and your rights regarding personal data.**

If you would like to exercise your data protection rights — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data — please use the information below to submit your request.

## How to Submit a Request

### By Email
Please send your request to: **privacy@bunyanhome.com**

Please include the following information in your request:
1. Your full name and email address (as used when contacting us)
2. A clear description of your request (access, correction, deletion, or restriction)
3. Any relevant details that help us locate your data (e.g., approximate date of inquiry, company name)
4. A copy of your identification document (for verification purposes)

### Response Time
We will respond to all verified data subject requests within **30 days**. In complex cases, this period may be extended by up to 60 days, and we will notify you in advance.

### No Fee Required
We do not charge a fee for processing data subject requests, unless your requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you |
| **Rectification** | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data |
| **Erasure** | Request deletion of your personal data |
| **Restriction** | Request limitation of how we process your data |
| **Portability** | Request transfer of your data to another organization |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interests |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw previously given consent at any time |

## Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
