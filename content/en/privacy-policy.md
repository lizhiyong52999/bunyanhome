## 7. Your Data Protection Rights

### 7.1 Rights Under GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of how we use your data.
- **Right to Data Portability**: Request transfer of your data to another controller.
- **Right to Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

### 7.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

California residents have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information is being collected and how it is used.
- **Delete** personal information collected from you (with certain exceptions).
- **Opt-Out** of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell your data).
- **Non-Discrimination** for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a data request, please contact: privacy@bunyanhome.com

### 7.3 Rights Under UAE PDPL

Users in the United Arab Emirates have the right to:

- Request access to their personal data.
- Request correction or update of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of data when it is no longer necessary.
- Withdraw consent for data processing.
- Request restriction or limitation of processing.

### 7.4 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us at:  
**privacy@bunyanhome.com**

We will respond to all verified data subject requests within **30 days**. In complex cases, this period may be extended by up to 60 days, with prior notification.

## 8. Cookies

### 8.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us improve your browsing experience and understand how our site is used.

### 8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Essential Cookies** | Required for basic website functionality | Session |
| **Preference Cookies** | Remember your language and display preferences | 1 year |
| **Analytics Cookies** | Help us understand website usage patterns | 1 year |

### 8.3 Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through our cookie consent banner, which appears on your first visit. You may also adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though this may affect website functionality.

## 9. Data Retention
